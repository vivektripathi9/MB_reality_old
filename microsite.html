Step away from the rush of the city and into a place where life feels a little more open, a little more peaceful, and a lot more connected to nature.

Lap of Nature is a thoughtfully planned residential plotted community in the Whitefield–East Bengaluru corridor, created for those who want to build their dream home amidst greenery while staying connected to the city's most important destinations.

Your Plot. Your Home. Your Way of Living.

Where the Air is Fresh and the Mind is Clear

Life is better when you have room to breathe. At Lap of Nature, thoughtfully planned villa plots come together with landscaped green spaces, recreational zones and lifestyle amenities to create a community designed around the way modern families want to live.
